Portfolio 9: My Summer Read/Watch List

Overview:

Goal:

My best friends Laura and Gabby have great taste! With summer break around the corner, I want to select some good books to read and movies to watch during my time off from school. My goal for this piece is to develop a tool for choosing my next book/movie using my own preferences and data from Laura’s Letterboxd account and Gabby’s GoodReads account to generate recommendations.

Data:

I entered Laura and Gabby’s reviews into a spreadsheet that includes the following variables:

  • Title (the title of the movie/book)
  • Rating (my friend’s rating of each work from 1 to 5 stars)
  • Type (movie or book)
  • Genre (these are broad, somewhat imprecise categories. Options for movies are Comedy, Drama, Family, Thriller/Horror, and Romance. Options for books are Literary Fiction, Historical, Sci-Fi, Thriller, and Nonfiction/Memoir).
  • Length (in minutes for movies and in pages for books)

I recorded this information for 30 movies and 30 books.

Product

A simple set of codes that returns a recommendation for me based on my preferences for the type of work, the genre, the length, and my friends’ ratings.

Saving my progress

As I watch/read the recommended movies/books, I want to be able to keep track of what I have watched/read already so I can continuing narrowing my recommendations down to works that are new to me. I also want to save my own ratings of the movies and books to compare to my friends’ ratings. I will create new variables in my dataframe to save my progress and filter my future recommendations:

recs <- recs %>%
  mutate(watched = 0) %>%
  mutate(my_rating = 0)
