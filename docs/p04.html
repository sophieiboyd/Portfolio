Portfolio 4: Using Changes in Perceptions of Teens Over Time to Predict Behavioral Intentions

Overview:

Goal:

As a second part of the teen counterstereotyping project, I examined whether changes in the participants’ perceptions of teens predicted their own behavioral intentions. I found that changes in perceptions of teens corresponded to positive behavioral intentions. For example, decreased perceptions of teens’ antisocial behavior predicted lower intentions to engage in antisocial behavior, and increased perceptions of teens’ positive family relationships predicted greater intentions to express gratitude toward a family member. The goal of this piece is to find an effective way to visualize the associations between changes in views of teens and behavioral intentions.

Data:

For this piece, I will be using the same dataset from portfolio pieces 2 and 3, but with new central variables. I will be using change scores to represent changes in the participants’ ratings of teens on each attribute from pre- to post-test. I will also be using measures of their behavioral intentions recorded at post-test.

Product:

A series of scatterplots to display the associations between change scores and personal behavioral intentions, culminating in a single figure that combines all plots.

Interpretation:

Improvements in views of teens predicted positive behavioral intentions. (Decreases on negative attributes predicted lower negative behavioral intentions, and increases on positive attributes predicted higher positive intentions).

Sources:

https://r-graph-gallery.com/package/patchwork.html
